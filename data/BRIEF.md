# Lakeside Medical Group — Patient Scheduling Agent

Lakeside Medical Group is a multi-specialty practice with three locations in the
Chicago area. Their front desk is overwhelmed with phone calls for appointments.

They want a voice agent that patients can call to book appointments. Patients
often don't know which kind of doctor they need.

Build it. The attached data is theirs.

## Files

- `providers.csv` — doctors, specialties, locations, languages, insurance
- `patients.csv` — existing patients
- `slots.csv` — appointment slots for the next three weeks
- `appointments.csv` — currently booked appointments
- `clinic_rules.json` — locations, specialties, eligibility rules, policies

## Notes
